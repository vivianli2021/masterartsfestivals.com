What Is NASA SLI?

The NASA Student Launch Initiative (SLI) is a hands-on engineering challenge where students design, build, test, and fly high-powered rockets.

Teams use a structured design process to develop a complete flight system, including the rocket, payload, recovery system, and launch operations. The program gives students real experience in engineering, safety, testing, and teamwork.
