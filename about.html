Who We Are

Built to Launch

MasterArtsFestivals is a student rocketry team competing in the American Rocketry Challenge — designing, simulating, and launching fully 3D-printed rockets.

Our Mission

MasterArtsFestivals designs, simulates, and launches fully 3D-printed rockets for the American Rocketry Challenge. We blend engineering discipline with fast iteration: every launch gives us data, every data point sharpens the next build.

From CAD to launch pad, we focus on clean design, reliable recovery, and teamwork under pressure. Our goal is simple: build smarter rockets and fly them better every cycle.
